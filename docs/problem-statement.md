# Problem statement

## Use Case

Demand Sensing & Replenishment Planning for MedCare Pharma

## Data Source

Synthetic data of –

- Historical & sensed demand (by SKU, by region)
- Current inventory with batch/expiry details
- Warehouse capacity & lead times
- Distributor order patterns
- Promotional/seasonal calendar

## Use case Description / Problem Statement

MedCare Pharma faces flu-season demand spikes (+60%) causing stock-outs of critical SKUs in Tier-2 cities, while metro DCs sit on excess near-expiry stock. Siloed regional warehouses and expiry-blind allocation drive both wastage and lost sales.

Need: sense near-term demand shifts, allocate stock with expiry awareness, and balance safety stock vs. wastage across DCs.

## Objective / Outcome

To build a demand-sensing and replenishment plan for MedCare Pharma that
keeps critical SKUs available while minimizing expiry-driven wastage across the
network.

Expected Outcomes:

- Improve short-term forecast accuracy using sensing signals (promotions,
  seasonality, leading indicators).
- Design expiry-aware allocation logic to reduce write-offs.
- Reduce stock-outs of critical SKUs during demand surges.

  simulation
- Recommend a review cadence/escalation process for shortage situations
